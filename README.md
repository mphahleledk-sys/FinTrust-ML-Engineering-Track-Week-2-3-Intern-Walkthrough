# FinTrust-ML-Engineering-Track-Week-2-Intern-Walkthrough

AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab
FinTrust Digital Bank — Project Brief
Project: FinTrust Financial Intelligence & Digital Banking Support Solution
Project Duration: 4 Weeks Project Type: Multidisciplinary Data & AI Experience Lab
Organisation: FinTrust Digital Bank (fictional)
Important: FinTrust is a fictional organisation. All customers, transactions, labels and business information used in this project
are synthetic and intended for educational purposes only.
1. Business Scenario
FinTrust Digital Bank is a growing digital banking organisation serving customers through mobile and online
banking channels. As its customer base grows, FinTrust is collecting increasing amounts of customer and
transaction data. However, the organisation needs a stronger way to turn this information into actionable business
intelligence and reliable digital solutions.
Management wants to better understand customer behaviour, transaction patterns and potential financial-risk
indicators, while also improving operational decision-making and digital customer support. FinTrust has therefore
assembled a multidisciplinary team of Data Analysts, Data Scientists, ML Engineers, Generative AI practitioners
and Project Managers.
2. Business Problem
FinTrust currently has customer and transaction information, but lacks an integrated solution that can:
• Provide clear visibility into customer and transaction behaviour.
• Identify potentially unusual or higher-risk transaction patterns.
• Generate predictive insights that can support risk-related decision-making.
• Operationalise analytical or machine-learning outputs into a usable workflow.
• Provide customers with reliable digital support based on approved FinTrust information.
• Bring these capabilities together into a coherent business solution.
3. Project Objective
Primary Objective
To develop an integrated data and AI solution that helps FinTrust Digital Bank understand customer and
transaction behaviour, identify potential financial-risk patterns, support operational decision-making and improve
digital customer support.
Supporting Objectives
• Analyse financial and customer data.
• Identify meaningful business trends and patterns.
• Develop relevant KPIs and business insights.
• Build and evaluate a predictive model for an appropriate risk-related problem.
• Develop an operational workflow around the model.
• Build a controlled GenAI customer-support capability.
• Apply appropriate testing and validation.
• Integrate outputs from multiple technical disciplines.
• Communicate findings and recommendations professionally.
4. Key Business Questions
Customer & Transaction Intelligence
• Who are FinTrust's major customer segments?
• How do transaction behaviours differ across customer groups?
• What transaction patterns are most important to management?
• Which products, channels or transaction types generate the greatest activity?
Risk & Predictive Intelligence
• What characteristics are associated with potentially risky transactions?
• Can historical transaction information be used to predict whether a transaction requires risk review?
• How well does the predictive model perform?
• Which factors contribute most to the model's predictions?
Customer Support & GenAI
• How can FinTrust provide faster answers to common customer questions?
• Can a GenAI assistant answer questions using only approved FinTrust information?
• How should unsupported or sensitive questions be handled?
• How can the assistant's responses be evaluated for accuracy and safety?
5. Project Scope
In Scope
• Data Analytics: data exploration, cleaning, KPI development, customer/transaction analysis, dashboarding and
business insights.
• Data Science: exploratory analysis, feature engineering, predictive modelling, model evaluation, error analysis
and interpretation.
• ML Engineering: model/pipeline integration, data/model workflow, API or service concepts, testing and technical
documentation.
• Generative AI: prompt engineering, knowledge integration, customer-support assistant,
RAG/knowledge-retrieval concepts, evaluation and guardrails.
• Project Management: planning, WBS, timeline management, dependency management, risk management,
coordination, integration and final delivery.
Out of Scope
• Processing real customer banking information.
• Connecting to real bank accounts or executing real financial transactions.
• Providing personalised financial or investment advice.
• Making real lending, fraud or account-opening decisions.
• Deploying a production banking system.
• Making regulatory or legal claims beyond the approved fictional project resources.
6. Multidisciplinary Team Structure
Track Primary Responsibility
Project Management Planning, coordination, risks, dependencies and delivery.
Data Analytics KPIs, dashboards, business analysis and insights.
Data Science Predictive modelling and model evaluation.
ML Engineering Model/pipeline operationalisation and technical integration.
Generative AI Knowledge-based customer-support capability, evaluation and safety.
Team Principle: Each intern remains individually accountable for their own track deliverables. Formal cross-track
collaboration begins in Week 2, after interns have produced tangible outputs.
7. Expected Final Solution
The team is expected to produce a coherent FinTrust Digital Banking Intelligence & Support Solution. The exact
implementation may vary by track, but each component must have a clear purpose and relationship to the overall
business problem.
Illustrative flow: Customer & Transaction Data ® Analytics & KPIs ® Predictive Risk Intelligence ® ML
Workflow/Service ® Knowledge-Based GenAI Support ® Integrated FinTrust Solution
8. Four-Week Project Journey
Week Phase Core Focus
Week 1 Understand & Plan Business understanding, resource review, data/knowledge assessment,
objectives and individual planning.
Week 2 Analyse & Prepare Analysis, preparation, initial development and formal cross-track collaboration.
Week 3 Develop & Integrate Advanced development, cross-track integration and validation.
Week 4 Test, Refine & Present Testing, refinement, final integration and individual presentation.
9. Project Success Criteria
• Business Understanding: The solution addresses a clearly defined FinTrust business problem.
• Data & Technical Quality: Data, analysis, models and technical components are appropriately developed and
documented.
• Integration: Outputs from different tracks are connected and actually used.
• Validation: The team tests its work and demonstrates improvements based on findings.
• Responsible AI/Data Use: The team recognises limitations, avoids unsupported claims and uses synthetic data
appropriately.
• Communication: The solution can be explained clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
• Portfolio Readiness: Work is documented and presented to a professional standard.
10. Data & AI Governance Principles
All interns must follow these principles throughout the project:
• Use only the supplied synthetic datasets and approved project resources unless an assignment explicitly permits
another resource.
• Do not treat the synthetic Risk_Review_Flag as a real fraud determination.
• Do not provide personalised financial, investment or legal advice.
• The GenAI assistant must not invent FinTrust policies, fees, limits, timelines or procedures.
• Where approved information is unavailable, the assistant should acknowledge the limitation and follow the
defined escalation approach.
• Document important assumptions, limitations and decisions.
11. Final Portfolio Expectations
Every intern will retain individual ownership of their track contribution. At the end of the project, each intern will
submit an individual 5–10 minute recorded presentation explaining their contribution, how it connected to the wider
solution, key results, limitations and lessons learned.
